# License Guide

This is the plain-English guide to the repository license.

The binding terms are in the root [LICENSE](../LICENSE) file.

## What Is Allowed

You can:

- self-host Approva Open Core
- modify it for your own use
- use it commercially
- include it inside a larger product or service
- deploy and operate it for a specific customer as part of consulting, managed services, or
  enterprise delivery work

## What Is Not Allowed Without A Commercial License

You cannot:

- offer Approva itself as a standalone hosted SaaS product
- build a competing approval platform where Approva is the primary value
- white-label or OEM it without permission
- remove attribution or present Approva as your own product

## Attribution

Visible attribution is required.

Use:

`Powered by Approva`

or equivalent wording that clearly credits Approva.

## Examples

✔ SaaS with Approva inside

- Your main product is an AI operations platform and Approva is one subsystem used for approval
  checkpoints.

✔ Consulting / deployment

- You deploy and operate Approva for a named customer on their cloud account, on your managed
  infrastructure, or as part of an implementation engagement.

✔ VPS templates

- You publish a VPS or infrastructure template that helps customers self-host Approva with the
  required attribution intact.

❌ SaaS that IS Approva

- You host Approva as the main product and sell access to it as a standalone approval service.

❌ White-label approval platform

- You remove the Approva name and present the product as your own approval infrastructure offering.
